ABOUT SUBMISSIONS

Your contact stays private.

Your description and contact are stored on the GPU server so the workshop team can review your submission and reply. Contact details are not shown in the public catalogue.

A project is published after review, with its title, description, maker’s name, and link. New ideas and requests to revive projects stay in the private submission list.

To correct or remove a submission, send a new request using the same contact and describe the change. We use Google Analytics to measure visits; it may store analytics cookies. Submission contact details are not sent to analytics.
